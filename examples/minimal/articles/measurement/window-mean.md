# Computing a window mean

A window mean summarizes equally weighted observations in the same unit.
It does not describe their variation or imply that future samples will match.

## Calculation

For the synthetic values 2, 4 and 6 degrees, the sum is 12 degrees and the count
is 3. The mean is therefore 4 degrees. Unequal sampling intervals require a
separate choice of weighting.
