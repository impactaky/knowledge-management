# Article curation

**Understanding**: An explanation whose evidence, assumptions and unknowns are
clear enough to review.

**Article**: A self-contained unit that restores one coherent understanding.

**Draft**: A candidate awaiting human review, kept in the selected store's root
`drafts/` directory. It is neither adopted nor search-published.

**Stock**: Explicit approval of a presented draft's content, destination and
claims, followed by moving it into `articles/<theme>/` and committing it.

**Replacement**: A reviewed update to understanding or provenance at the same slug.

**Revision**: A reviewed expression change without changing understanding.

**Executable article**: A marimo source notebook with static explanatory prose;
frozen HTML is a derived view, never the original.
